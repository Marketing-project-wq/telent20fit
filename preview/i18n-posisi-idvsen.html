JUDGES HYBRID RACE
Bertugas menilai peserta di station-station kompetisi hybrid race (kombinasi lari dan gerakan fungsional), memastikan setiap station dijalankan sesuai aturan.
Lihat Detail ⌄
📋 Jobdesk
Menilai dan mengawasi peserta di station tertentu sesuai format hybrid race (misalnya sled push, wall ball, rowing, dll), memastikan repetisi/gerakan sah, memberi penalti bila ada pelanggaran, mencatat hasil penilaian sebelum peserta lanjut ke station berikutnya.
✅ Persyaratan
Minimal usia 17 tahun, teliti dan disiplin, mampu berdiri/aktif bergerak selama bertugas, diutamakan familiar dengan format functional training/hybrid race tapi tidak wajib.

JUDGES RUNNING

📋 Jobdesk
Mengawasi peserta di checkpoint/titik tugas yang ditentukan, memastikan peserta mengikuti rute dan aturan lomba dengan benar, mencatat pelanggaran (misalnya cutting course) bila ditemukan, berkoordinasi dengan tim timing/marshal terkait hasil pengawasan.
✅ Persyaratan
Minimal usia 17 tahun, teliti dan disiplin, mampu berdiri/berada di titik tugas dalam waktu cukup lama, diutamakan familiar dengan event lari tapi tidak wajib.

JUDGES HYBRID RACE
Judges participants at the stations of a hybrid-race competition (a mix of running and functional movements), making sure every station runs by the rules.
View Detail ⌄
📋 Jobdesk
Judge and supervise participants at an assigned station per the hybrid-race format (e.g. sled push, wall ball, rowing, etc.), verify that reps/movements are valid, apply penalties for any violation, and record the results before a participant moves on to the next station.
✅ Requirements
At least 17 years old, thorough and disciplined, able to stand and stay active throughout the shift; familiarity with functional-training / hybrid-race formats is preferred but not required.

📋 Jobdesk
Monitor participants at an assigned checkpoint/post, make sure they follow the route and race rules correctly, record any violation (e.g. cutting the course) when found, and coordinate with the timing/marshal team on your observations.
✅ Requirements
At least 17 years old, thorough and disciplined, able to stand / stay at your post for fairly long periods; familiarity with running events is preferred but not required.

📋 Jobdesk
Direct participants along the course, keep the route safe and clear, help with light logistics (moving equipment between points), and respond quickly to on-site needs.
